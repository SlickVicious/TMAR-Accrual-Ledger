## 5. Coverage gaps (FileCabinet data not yet reflected in a sheet)

1. **13,809 raw bank transactions** exist as CSVs but are only *summarized* in the cash-flow
   tabs and *partially* journalized (229 JEs vs ~13,809 transactions). The Journal currently
   represents recurring obligations (subscriptions/utilities/debt), not the full bank-activity
   ledger. Full bank → Journal posting is the remaining lift.
2. **Truist is thin** — 10 rows in the CSV vs the trust account being the primary operating
   account; Truist statements likely under-extracted.
3. **`Ledgers` (TLE book)** has 17 monthly rows (BOA/PNC/CapOne periods) but the Truist trust
   sub-ledger is absent.
4. **Valuations** reference credit-report charges (VAL-001/002 = Capital One, Continental
   Finance) but the `09-Valuations/` FileCabinet dir holds only 3 images — the valuation
   backing documents live elsewhere (Credit-Reports/, 06-Account-Register/).
