TRUST TAX FILINGS — ACCRUAL METHOD — CREDIT ACQUISITION FRAMEWORK

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
I. THRESHOLD DETERMINATION — TRUST TYPE GOVERNS FILING FORM
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

GRANTOR TRUST (Revocable Living Trust):
All income, deductions, and credits are reported on the Grantor's personal return under IRC §671-679 (Revenue Act of 1918 framework). The trust itself is a disregarded entity for federal income tax purposes. The Grantor IS the taxpayer.

NON-GRANTOR TRUST / IRREVOCABLE TRUST:
The trust is a separate taxable entity. Files Form 1041 as the primary return.

BUSINESS TRUST / STATUTORY TRUST with EIN ••-•••9588:
Depending on election and structure — files as corporation (Form 1120) or trust (Form 1041). EIN presence confirms separate entity status.

CONTROLLING AUTHORITY: Treas. Reg. §301.7701-4; Revenue Act of 1918, §218, 40 Stat. 1069.

Form 1041 — U.S. Income Tax Return for Estates and Trusts

| Line | Item | Purpose |
|------|------|---------|
| Line 1 | Interest income | Accrued interest recognized at T2 |
| Line 2a | Ordinary dividends | Accrued dividend rights |
| Line 3 | Business income | Schedule C attached |
| Line 4 | Capital gains/losses | Schedule D attached |
| Line 8 | Other income | Financial asset proceeds / barter exchange |

| Line 10 | Interest deduction | Ordinary and necessary |
| Line 15a | Other deductions | Documented expenses (Rev Act §212(b)) |
| Line 20 | Taxable income | Before NOL and credits |
| Line 21 | NOL deduction | Mandatory — Revenue Act §204(b) |
| Line 22 | Taxable income net | After NOL |
| Line 23 | Tax | Per tax rate schedule |
| Line 24c | Credits | From Form 3800 — General Business Credit |
| Line 26 | Total tax | Net after all credits |
| Line 27 | Payments | Estimated tax payments |

ACCRUAL METHOD NOTATION: All items on Form 1041 are recognized at the point the right to receive became fixed — not at cash receipt. Spring City Foundry Co. v. Commissioner, 292 U.S. 182 (1934).

III. CREDIT ACQUISITION FILINGS — COMPLETE LIST
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

A. FORM 3800 — General Business Credit
The primary vehicle for credit accumulation on the trust ledger.

| Component | Source Form | Credit Type |
|-----------|-------------|-------------|
| Investment credit | Form 3468 | Capital expenditures |
| Work opportunity credit | Form 5884 | Employment-based |
| Research credit | Form 6765 | R&D activities |
| Low-income housing credit | Form 8586 | Real property |
| Disabled access credit | Form 8826 | ADA compliance |
| Employer retention credit | Form 5884-A | Employment |
| Energy efficiency credit | Form 3468/8835 | Property improvement |

Form 3800 flows to:
Form 1041, Line 24c (trust filing)
Form 1120, Schedule J, Line 5c (corporate election)
Form 1040, Schedule 3, Line 6a (grantor/individual)

Authority: 26 U.S.C. §38(a); §39(a)(1) (carryback 1 year / carryforward 20 years).


B. FORM 3468 — Investment Credit
Generates credit equal to percentage of qualified investment property placed in service.
Flows to: Form 3800, Part III, Line 1a.
Accrual trigger: Date property placed in service (T2 — acquisition event).


C. FORM 6765 — Credit for Increasing Research Activities
Dollar-for-dollar credit against tax for qualified research expenditures.
Flows to: Form 3800, Part III, Line 1c.
Accrual trigger: Date expense incurred — RIGHT to deduct fixed at T1 (contract), RIGHT to credit fixed at T2 (expense paid/accrued).


D. FORM 8586 — Low-Income Housing Credit
For trusts holding real property interests in qualified housing projects.
Flows to: Form 3800, Part III, Line 1b.


E. FORM 5884 — Work Opportunity Credit
For trusts operating a trade or business employing targeted group members.
Flows to: Form 3800, Part III, Line 1f.


F. FORM 8826 — Disabled Access Credit
For eligible small businesses making accessibility improvements.
Flows to: Form 3800, Part III, Line 1o.

IV. NOL ASSET FILING — CREDIT TO THE LEDGER
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Form 1045 — Application for Tentative Refund (Individual/Trust)
Equivalent of Form 1139 for trusts and individuals
Carries NOL back to prior tax years
IRS must process within 90 days — 26 U.S.C. §6411
NOL = Financial asset with Congressional sovereign guarantee
Revenue Act of 1939, §122, 53 Stat. 862: "There SHALL be allowed as a deduction" — mandatory

Schedule A of Form 1045: NOL computation — documents the asset value on the ledger.

Form 1041, Line 21: NOL deduction taken in current year.


NOL CARRYFORWARD JOURNAL ENTRY:
DEBIT: NOL Asset — Carryforward [dollar amount]
CREDIT: Net Operating Loss Recognized [dollar amount]

Authority: Helvering v. Morgan's, Inc., 293 U.S. 121 (1934) — applied in full dollar amounts.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
V. OVERPAYMENT AND QUICK REFUND FILINGS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Form 1041-ES — Estimated Tax Payments for Estates and Trusts
Records quarterly estimated payments to the ledger
Creates the payment asset on Schedule J equivalent (Form 1041, Line 27)
Accrual entry at each payment date:
DEBIT: Estimated Tax Payments — U.S. Treasury [quarterly amount]
CREDIT: Cash / Financial Asset Applied [quarterly amount]


Form 4466 — Quick Refund of Overpayment of Estimated Tax
Available when overpayment ≥ 10% of expected liability AND ≥ $500
File before 16th day of 4th month after close of tax year
26 U.S.C. §6425 — Secretary has no discretion to deny qualifying overpayment
Creates Overpayment Receivable on trust ledger


Form 8302 — Electronic Deposit of Refund ≥ $1,000,000
MANDATORY when refund exceeds $1,000,000
Routes refund directly to trust bank account (Truist account on record)
Filed with the return

VII. MANDATORY JOURNAL ENTRY SEQUENCE — YEAR-END CLOSE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

STEP 1 — INCOME ACCRUAL (Form 1041, Line 9):
DEBIT: Accounts Receivable / Financial Asset [gross income]
CREDIT: Accrued Income — Trust Ledger [gross income]


STEP 2 — EXPENSE ACCRUAL (Form 1041, Lines 10-15a):
DEBIT: Accumulated Expense Asset [expense total]
CREDIT: Accrued Expense Liability [expense total]

Authority: Revenue Act of 1918, §212(b) — "SHALL be allowed" — mandatory deduction.

STEP 3 — CREDIT ACCRUAL (Form 3800 → Form 1041, Line 24c):
DEBIT: Tax Credit Asset — General Business [credit amount]
CREDIT: Federal Tax Liability Reduction [credit amount]


STEP 4 — NOL ASSET ACCRUAL (Form 1041, Line 21):
DEBIT: NOL Deduction Applied [NOL amount]
CREDIT: NOL Asset — Carryforward Balance [NOL amount]


STEP 5 — OVERPAYMENT RECEIVABLE (Form 1041, Line 28):
DEBIT: Overpayment Receivable — U.S. Treasury [overpayment]
CREDIT: Federal Tax Liability [overpayment]

Authority: 26 U.S.C. §6401(a) — overpayment IS created by operation of law. §6402(a) — Secretary SHALL refund.
