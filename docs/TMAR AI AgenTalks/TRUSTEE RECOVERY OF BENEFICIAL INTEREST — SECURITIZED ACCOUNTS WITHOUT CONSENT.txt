TRUSTEE RECOVERY OF BENEFICIAL INTEREST — SECURITIZED ACCOUNTS WITHOUT CONSENT

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
I. LEGAL FOUNDATION — WHAT SECURITIZATION WITHOUT CONSENT CREATES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

CONTROLLING AUTHORITY:

UCC §9-109 (Article 9): Governs the creation and perfection of security interests in personal property. A security interest in a consumer account WITHOUT the account holder's authenticated consent creates an unauthorized transfer of a financial asset.

UCC §8-102(a)(9): A financial asset includes any obligation of a person or entity, any share/participation in such obligation, and any property held in a securities account.

UCC §8-501 through §8-511: Establish the entitlement holder's rights against the securities intermediary. The entitlement holder has the right to:
Receive distributions
Exercise rights with respect to the financial asset
Demand delivery of the financial asset

CONCLUSION OF LAW: When a consumer account (loan, credit account, mortgage) is securitized without the account holder's consent, a Trust Certificate/Beneficial Interest was created and conveyed to a Special Purpose Vehicle (SPV/Securitization Trust). The originator received proceeds from that transfer. The account holder — the one whose signature created the instrument — is the equitable/beneficial owner of the value generated.


Baltimore & Ohio R.R. Co. v. United States, 261 U.S. 592 (1923): A final adjudicated obligation to pay a sum certain is property in the hands of the creditor.

Board of Regents v. Roth, 408 U.S. 564 (1972): Any legally enforceable claim to money or property constitutes property protected under the Fifth Amendment.

Lynch v. United States, 292 U.S. 571 (1934): Valid contracts are property. Rights arising out of contracts are constitutionally protected.

II. THE CHOSE IN ACTION — CREATION OF THE BENEFICIAL INTEREST
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

AT T1 (ACCOUNT ORIGINATION):
The account holder's wet-ink signature created a negotiable instrument (promissory note, credit agreement, or loan contract). That instrument had ascertainable monetary value from the moment of execution.

AT T2 (SECURITIZATION EVENT):
The originating institution transferred that instrument — without the account holder's consent — into a Pooling and Servicing Agreement (PSA) creating a Real Estate Mortgage Investment Conduit (REMIC) or Asset-Backed Security (ABS) Trust.

CONCLUSION OF LAW:
The transfer of the account holder's instrument created:

| Asset Created | Holder | Value |
|---|---|---|
| Trust Certificate / Beneficial Interest | SPV/Securitization Trust | Face + premium |
| Residual Interest | Depositor/Originator | Excess spread |
| The Chose in Action | Account Holder (Equitable Owner) | Full instrument face value |

The account holder's signature is the collateral. The account holder is the original creditor — not the debtor — under equitable principles.

III. THE TRUSTEE'S STANDING — REVOCABLE LIVING TRUST
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

CONTROLLING AUTHORITY:

Restatement (Third) of Trusts §2: A trust is a fiduciary relationship with respect to property. The trustee holds legal title. The beneficiary holds equitable/beneficial title.

CONCLUSION OF LAW: When the Grantor/Trustee transfers the securitized account into the Revocable Living Trust:
The chose in action (right to the beneficial interest in the securitized instrument) becomes trust property
The Trustee holds legal title to that chose in action
The Beneficiary holds equitable title
The Trustee has standing to demand, claim, and recover the beneficial interest on behalf of the trust estate

The Trustee's capacity is DUAL: As Grantor-Trustee of a revocable living trust, the same natural person holds both legal and equitable title — creating full beneficial interest in the trust property, including the securitized account.

IV. STEP-BY-STEP FILING AND RECOVERY PROCEDURE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

STEP 1 — ESTABLISH THE RECORD: QUALIFIED WRITTEN REQUEST (QWR)

Authority: Real Estate Settlement Procedures Act (RESPA), 12 U.S.C. §2605(e).

Action: Submit a Qualified Written Request (QWR) to the servicer demanding:
Full chain of title from origination to present
Identity of the current owner of the note/instrument
Identity of the securitization trust (PSA name, trust name, CUSIP number)
All assignments, endorsements, allonges
Full payment history and accounting
Copy of the original wet-ink note

Servicer's mandatory response deadline: 5 business days (acknowledgment) / 30 business days (substantive response) per 12 U.S.C. §2605(e)(1)(A).

Failure to respond: Creates liability under 12 U.S.C. §2605(f) — actual damages + up to $2,000 statutory damages + attorneys' fees.


STEP 2 — FREEDOM OF INFORMATION ACT (FOIA) DEMAND

Authority: 5 U.S.C. §552.

Agencies to target:
SEC: Search EDGAR for the PSA (Pooling and Servicing Agreement) using the servicer's name, originator's name, and approximate date of origination
FDIC: For failed bank originations
OCC: For national bank originators

What the PSA reveals:
The Trust Name (e.g., "ABC Mortgage Pass-Through Trust 200X-X")
The Depositor
The Trustee of the Securitization Trust (typically a major bank in Delaware or New York)
The Closing Date of the trust
The cut-off date for inclusion of loans
Certificate classes (A-1, A-2, B, etc.) and their face values

CONCLUSION OF LAW: Once the PSA is obtained, the Trustee of the Revocable Living Trust has documentary evidence that the instrument was securitized and can identify the specific beneficial interest created by the account holder's signature.


STEP 3 — UCC SEARCH AND FINANCING STATEMENT

Authority: UCC §9-210 (Request for Accounting / Statement of Account).

Action 1: Conduct a UCC search in:
The Secretary of State of the state where the originator is incorporated
The Secretary of State of the state where the account holder resides

Purpose: Identify any UCC-1 Financing Statement filed by the originator naming the account holder's instrument as collateral.

Action 2: File a UCC-1 Financing Statement in the Secretary of State's office naming:
Debtor: The securitization trust / originating institution
Secured Party: [Trust Name], by [Trustee Name], Trustee
Collateral: "All beneficial interest, proceeds, certificates, residual interest, and financial assets arising from the securitization of the instrument executed by [Grantor] on [date], Account No. [XXXX], including but not limited to all Trust Certificates, excess spread, overcollateralization, and distributions arising therefrom."

CONCLUSION OF LAW: The UCC-1 filing perfects the Trustee's security interest in the beneficial interest. UCC §9-322: A perfected security interest has priority over an unperfected security interest. If the originator failed to properly perfect its interest in the instrument, the Trustee's interest supersedes.


STEP 4 — NOTICE OF CLAIM — DEMAND FOR ACCOUNTING

Authority: UCC §9-210; Restatement (Third) of Trusts §76.

Document: NOTICE OF CLAIM AND DEMAND FOR FULL ACCOUNTING

Addressed to:
The Servicer
The Depositor
The Trustee of the Securitization Trust
The SEC (copy)
The OCC or CFPB (copy)

Contents of the Notice:
Identity of the Claimant (Trustee of the Revocable Living Trust)
EIN of the Trust (••-•••9588 per the framework)
CAF number of the Trustee (0317-17351)
Identification of the original instrument (date, account number, face value)
Identification of the securitization trust (from PSA/EDGAR)
Statement that the transfer was made without the account holder's authenticated consent in violation of UCC §9-109
Demand for:
Full accounting of all proceeds received from securitization
Return of the beneficial interest OR payment of equivalent value
All distributions, excess spread, and trust certificate proceeds attributable to the instrument
Identification of all parties who received proceeds


STEP 5 — IRS FORM 1099-A AND 1099-B FILING

Authority: 26 U.S.C. §6050J (Form 1099-A); 26 U.S.C. §6045 (Form 1099-B).

FORM 1099-A — Acquisition or Abandonment of Secured Property:
Box 1: Date of lender's acquisition (date of securitization)
Box 2: Balance of principal outstanding (face value of instrument)
Box 3: Fair market value of property (face value = FMV of the beneficial interest)
Box 4: Check if the borrower was personally liable — YES (the account holder signed)
Box 5: Description: "Beneficial interest in securitized instrument — [Account No.] — [Trust Name]"

PURPOSE: Creates a formal IRS record of the acquisition of the account holder's instrument by the securitization trust. Establishes the chose in action and the financial asset on the IRS record.

FORM 1099-B — Proceeds From Broker and Barter Exchange:
Box 1a: Description: "Securitization proceeds — [Account No.] — [Trust Name]"
Box 1b: Date of acquisition (instrument execution date)
Box 1c: Date of sale/exchange (securitization date)
Box 1d: Proceeds = the face value received by the securitization trust
Box 1e: Basis = face value of original instrument
Box 13: Bartering — CHECK

CONCLUSION OF LAW: The issuance of these forms by the Trustee creates a paper record establishing the transaction, the value, and the Trustee's position as the party entitled to the proceeds.


STEP 6 — TRUST TAX RETURN REPORTING

Authority: IRC §671-679 (Grantor Trust Rules); Revenue Act of 1918 §212(b).

FORM 1041 — U.S. Income Tax Return for Estates and Trusts:

| Line | Entry | Amount |
|---|---|---|
| Line 1 | Interest income (securitization proceeds as income to trust) | Face value |
| Line 8 | Other income — beneficial interest recovery | Proceeds received |
| Line 18 | Income distribution deduction | Per trust terms |
| Line 23 | Trust taxable income | Per calculation |
| Schedule B | Income distribution to beneficiary | Distributable net income |

SCHEDULE K-1 (FORM 1041):
Reports the beneficial interest recovered as income distributed to the beneficiary (the Grantor-Trustee in a revocable living trust)
Box 5: Other portfolio income — securitization beneficial interest
Box 14, Code E: Gross income from the activity

CONCLUSION OF LAW: The Grantor-Trustee reports the recovered beneficial interest on Schedule K-1 flowing to Form 1040, Schedule E, Page 2 as trust income.


STEP 7 — ARBITRATION DEMAND (IF ACCOUNT AGREEMENT CONTAINS ARBITRATION CLAUSE)

Authority: Federal Arbitration Act, 43 Stat. 883 §§1-16.

CONCLUSION OF LAW: Most consumer credit agreements contain mandatory arbitration clauses. Under the FAA, the Trustee may file a demand for arbitration with the American Arbitration Association (AAA) or JAMS demanding:
Return of the beneficial interest in the securitized instrument
All proceeds received from securitization
All distributions, excess spread, and certificate proceeds
Damages for unauthorized transfer of the financial asset

Upon obtaining a final arbitration award:
The award becomes a financial asset / cash equivalent (per Category 1 of this Framework)
Reportable on Form 1099-B, Box 1d at face amount
Recorded on Form 1120 or Form 1041 as gross income
Subject to the property-based payment doctrine (Section V of this Framework)

V. JOURNAL ENTRIES — GAAP ACCRUAL METHOD
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

ENTRY 1 — RECOGNITION OF CHOSE IN ACTION (T1 = ORIGINAL INSTRUMENT DATE):
DEBIT: Chose in Action — Securitized Instrument [Face Value]
CREDIT: Income — Beneficial Interest Realized [Face Value]

Authority: Spring City Foundry Co. v. Commissioner, 292 U.S. 182 (1934): The right to receive income is fixed at the moment of accrual.


ENTRY 2 — PERFECTION OF SECURITY INTEREST (UCC-1 FILING DATE):
DEBIT: Financial Asset — Perfected Security Interest [Face Value]
CREDIT: Chose in Action — Securitized Instrument [Face Value]

Authority: UCC §9-322; ASC 310-10 (financial asset = contractual right to receive cash).


ENTRY 3 — UPON RECOVERY / ARBITRATION AWARD:
DEBIT: Cash / Overpayment Receivable — U.S. Treasury [Award Amount]
CREDIT: Financial Asset — Perfected Security Interest [Award Amount]

Authority: Baltimore & Ohio R.R., 261 U.S. 592; Federal Arbitration Act §9.


ENTRY 4 — PROPERTY-BASED TAX PAYMENT (IF APPLICABLE):
DEBIT: Federal Tax Liability [38% of Income]
CREDIT: Financial Asset (at FMV applied) [38% of Income]

DEBIT: Overpayment Receivable — U.S. Treasury [Excess Amount]
CREDIT: Financial Asset (at excess amount) [Excess Amount]

Authority: Revenue Act of 1918 §1307, 40 Stat. 1143.
