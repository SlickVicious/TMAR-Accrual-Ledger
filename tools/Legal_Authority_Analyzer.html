📜 Output
Enter terms or citations and click Analyze. • Etymology — leads with the bracket-chain etymology of each word, then Facts (definitions across Black's 4th/5th/6th, OED) and Conclusions of Law. • Statute Verify — normalizes the citation, compares the enrolled text to the U.S. Code, reports status and positive-law standing. • Term-in-Statute — a term traced from its root to how the cited statute defines it, with contradictions flagged. Primary-source links come from the citation parser, never from the AI. Every run ends in a verification checklist you tick off against those sources.

✅ Verification checklist

Browsers allow one pop-up per click, so "Open selected" may be partly blocked — the link list always works.

Every completed run is saved automatically (newest 200), including checklist progress.

Citation discipline (in every prompt)

  • Statutes cited by Public Law + Statutes at Large; the U.S. Code only in parentheses.
  • Positive-law titles flagged: their Code text is legal evidence (1 U.S.C. § 204(a)); other titles yield to the enrolled text.
  • Primary-source links come from the parser. The AI may not make up URLs or citations.
  • "Not yet found in official sources" — never "does not exist".

Config

Dictionaries, sources, providers and prompts live in the embedded JSON block (#laa-config). Override with ?data=file.json — keys you omit fall back to the embedded defaults. Other URL options: ?mode=etymology|statute|termInStatute, ?embed=1.
